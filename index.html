Meet Salvatore

I'm Salvatore — a developer interested in building Discord bots, websites, and tools that make online communities more useful, secure, and enjoyable. I like exploring both the logic behind an application and the experience people have while using it.

My development interests span Python, Node.js, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS. I enjoy thinking through how a bot should behave, how commands should feel to use, how a website should guide its visitors, and how all the moving parts can work together reliably.

I'm especially drawn to all-in-one Discord bots: projects that bring moderation, security utilities, and everyday server tools together in one carefully organized experience. I also enjoy the creative side of development — visual identity, interface design, animation, and turning a rough concept into something with a clear personality.

For me, development is an ongoing mission. Every project is a chance to test an idea, solve a problem, learn a new technique, and raise the standard of what I build next.
